LAO — the independent third party between your LLM and your Runtime

LAO is ZWISERFIT's first product, open source. It sits between your LLM and your Runtime, aligned with your interests, only on your side: it picks the right model for every task, cuts wasted tokens, and anchors answers to your experience.

LAO 是 ZWISERFIT 的第一个产品，开源。它坐在你的 LLM 与 Runtime 之间，与你的利益一致，只站在你这一边：替你选对模型、堵住浪费的 token、把回答锚定在你的经验上。

Who watches the LLM and Runtime, so they stop burning your tokens? You need a trusted, independent third party. That's LAO.

谁来替你监督 LLM 和 Runtime，别让它们乱花你的 token？你需要一个可信的独立第三方——LAO 就是这个角色。

Your problem → what LAO does. 你的问题 → 我们怎么解决。

① Save money 省钱

Your problem: cache-miss requests are a small share of your tokens — but they eat most of your bill.

What LAO does: model selection + per-task matching + request optimization. Cache hit rate: 91.3% measured · pending official reconciliation 实测·待官方对账. Cache-miss requests: 8.5% of token volume, 56% of cost measured 实测·待官方对账. LAO plugs exactly that hole.

你的问题：缓存未命中的请求只占 token 量的 8.5%，却吃掉成本的 56%。LAO 的做法：模型选品＋按任务匹配＋请求优化，专门堵住这个洞。

② Memory 记忆

Your problem: every session starts blank; the experience you paid for walks out the door.

What LAO does: your experience settles in and compounds — the more you use it, the better it knows you. And it always stays yours.

你的问题：每次对话从零开始，花过的钱攒不下经验。LAO 的做法：你的经验沉淀下来，越用越懂你——并且始终是你的。

③ Answers anchored to you 不乱说话

Your problem: LLM answers are probabilistic — "maybe right" is not good enough when it spends your money.

What LAO does: two layers of certainty. Request side — intent lock: LAO optimizes how you say it, never what you mean. Answer side — experience anchor: LAO calibrates answers against your facts and your cognitive system, turning "maybe right" into "right against your tacit experience". If calibration fails, it honestly falls back to the LLM's raw answer — never forces one.

你的问题：LLM 的回答是概率性的，"可能对"不够。LAO 的做法：请求侧意图锁定——优化表达不改本意；回答侧经验锚定——校准不过关就诚实回退，绝不硬给。
