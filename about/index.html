Who we are 我们是谁

LAO is ZWISERFIT's first product, open source. Its position: an independent third party aligned with the user's interests — sitting between the LLM and the Runtime, watching the door for you, representing no vendor. Its proposition, in two lines: help you save money, and never talk loosely.

LAO 是 ZWISERFIT 的第一个产品，开源。定位：站在用户角度、与用户利益一致的独立第三方——坐在 LLM 与 Runtime 之间，替用户看门，不代表任何厂商。主张两句话：帮用户省钱，不乱说话。

Why we built it 为什么做这件事

"I hit these holes myself, so you don't have to." — burned tokens, models talking nonsense, pipelines breaking without warning, data scattered with no one knowing. Every one of these happened to us for real. LAO grew out of those holes, not out of a whiteboard.

"我走过的坑，不想大家继续走。"——烧掉的 token、胡说八道的模型、说断就断的管线、散落无人知的数据，这些坑我们都真实踩过。LAO 是从这些坑里长出来的，不是纸上设计的。
