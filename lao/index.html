How LAO saves your tokens

LAO — Lineage Anchored Ontology. An independent component between your LLM and your Runtime. The LLM stays smart; LAO keeps it honest — and cheap.

LAO是坐在你的LLM与Runtime之间的独立组件。LLM负责聪明，LAO负责可靠——还帮你省钱。

The three layers 三层工作

L1

Model selection · best value per task 模型选品

Safety first: unfit models excluded 安全过滤 Then speed 效率过滤 Then cost 成本过滤 Cache-aware request optimization 缓存感知请求优化
Output: the right model, the right price, for this task.
L2

Experience pipeline · your memory 经验沉淀

Experience settles in as you work 实时沉淀 Mistakes become permanent constraints 错误变约束 Context pruned to key anchors 上下文剪枝 Every step asks permission 每步都征求同意
Output: structured, reusable experience — always yours.
L3

Answer calibration · never bluff 回答校准

Intent lock: your meaning never altered 意图锁定 Answers calibrated against your facts 经验锚定 Calibration fails → honest fallback to raw answer 诚实回退
Output: "maybe right" becomes "right against your experience".
