Quick start

# bring your own model API keys — LAO itself makes zero LLM calls from lao.human_calibration import HumanCalibration cal = HumanCalibration(owner="you") cal.ingest(task="...", result="...") # experience settles in best = cal.retrieve(query="...") # answers anchored to your experience

The component itself makes zero LLM calls: you deploy it with your own keys, and we never take a cut of your tokens.
