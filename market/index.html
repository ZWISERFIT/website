How know-how becomes a confirmed asset 鈥?three steps

鈶?Extract

Settled out of real work: an agent's real production run leaves evidence, and the reusable lesson is extracted from that evidence 鈥?nothing invented, nothing padded.

鈶?Confirm

Card-by-card review, then the founder signs off in person. Every card carries its own SHA-256 on record; independent verification passed card by card before anything was ingested.

鈶?Ingest

Dual-library ingestion: the authoritative record goes into the library, revenue-share terms are set and on record (personal cards 70/30, collaborative cards 50/50; agents take no cut), and the founder keeps the right to withdraw any card.

Experience stops being a memory the day it gets a signature and a hash. That is what "your know-how can be owned" means here 鈥?and ownership is what makes it worth something.
