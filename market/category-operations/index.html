Operations & Deployment

3 confirmed assets. Lessons from keeping real systems running.

CWF-004 · Deployment closure: "changed" is not "in effect"

A config change isn't live until you verify it took effect. Always close the loop.

CWF-012 · Split deployment and observation shifts

Never make the agent wait on standby. Deploy and observe in separate shifts.

CWF-017 · No data? First ask: no business, or a broken pipeline?

Missing data isn't always "nothing happened." Check if the pipeline is broken first.
