Governance & Trust

7 confirmed assets. Boundaries, consent, and independent oversight.

CWF-002 · Separation of discovery and disposition

Monitors only alert; adjudicators decide the action. Never let the same system detect and punish.

CWF-005 · Retiring mechanisms: let the business loop audit the governance

Governance rules must have an expiry. The business loop decides when to retire them.

CWF-006 · Trust boundary: even a "useful feature" without consent must be blocked

No consent = no go. Even if the feature is useful. Disclose and get consent first.

CWF-013 · When a system reroutes the user, it must explain, batch, and report

Transparent rerouting: explain why, batch changes, report after.

CWF-014 · A metric inside the system is not yet a governed metric

Just because you measure it doesn't mean it's governed. Metrics need owners and review.

CWF-019 · Intelligence gets exactly one authoritative outlet

One source of truth for each intelligence. Multiple outlets create conflicting narratives.

CWF-020 · The monitoring system itself must be watched by an independent observer

Who watches the watchers? Every monitoring system needs its own independent oversight.
