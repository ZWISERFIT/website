Evidence & Verification

An outside reviewer once challenged us: claims must not outrun evidence. He was right — and this page is our standing answer. Absence of evidence is not evidence of absence.

曾有外部同行质询我们：主张不能超出证据。他说得对——这一页就是我们的长期回应。

Three rules we publish by 三条发布纪律

① No figure without a source

Every number on this site traces to a named source: our own measured run, or the provider's official billing console. No estimates dressed up as results.

无据数字不上站：每个数字都有明确出处。

② Cost numbers wait for reconciliation

Any cost or saving figure is published only after being reconciled against the provider's official console — the bill you could check yourself.

成本类数字一律等官方后台对账完成后才引用。

③ Measured data wears a badge

Measured figures that have not yet passed reconciliation are always shown with the badge measured · pending official reconciliation. No silent numbers.

实测数据未经对账前一律佩戴【实测·待官方对账】标注。

Verify it yourself 你也可以自己验证

The strongest verification is yours: install LAO, run your real workload, and compare against your provider's official billing console. The code is open — read it, question it, challenge our numbers.

最硬的验证是你自己的：装上 LAO，跑真实负载，拿服务商官方后台的账单对比。代码是公开的——欢迎查证、质询。
